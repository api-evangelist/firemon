# FireMon

> FireMon is a cybersecurity company providing firewall and security policy management across hybrid, on-premises, and cloud environments. Its platform normalizes and governs security policies spanning firewalls, cloud security groups, and microsegmentation, delivering compliance automation, policy optimization, change automation, and real-time risk insight. FireMon serves 1,700+ customers across 70+ countries. The Security Manager platform exposes a REST API, though it is delivered per-install (self-hosted/cloud appliance) rather than as a single public hosted endpoint.
